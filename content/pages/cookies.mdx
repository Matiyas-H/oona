**Last updated:** August 23, 2026

This site sets four cookies. One of them is optional, and it is off until you
say otherwise. There are no third-party trackers, no advertising pixels, and no
data broker relationships — everything below is set by us, stored by us, and
used by us alone.

## What we set

| Cookie | Purpose | Expires | Optional? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ov_attr` | Records which campaign, search, or link brought you here, so we can tell which marketing is worth paying for. Written once, on your first visit. | 90 days | **Yes** — off unless you accept |
| `ov_consent` | Remembers the answer you gave to the banner. | 1 year if you accept, 6 months if you decline | No |
| `__Host-authjs.csrf-token` | Protects sign-in forms against cross-site request forgery. | Session | No |
| `__Secure-authjs.callback-url` | Returns you to the right page after signing in. | Session | No |

Signing in sets an additional session cookie for as long as you stay signed in.

## Why the last three are not optional

European law asks what a cookie is *for*, not who owns the domain. A cookie is
exempt from consent only when it is strictly necessary for something you asked
for. Protecting a sign-in form and remembering where to send you afterwards
qualify. So does remembering that you said no — a record of your refusal is what
makes the refusal stick, and asking permission to store it would be circular.

Measuring marketing does not qualify, however first-party it is. That is why
`ov_attr` waits.

## What `ov_attr` contains

The campaign identifiers that were in the link you arrived on — `gclid` and any
`utm_*` parameters — plus the page you landed on, the site that referred you,
and a timestamp. If you arrive with none of those and no external referrer,
nothing is written at all.

It holds your **first** visit, not your most recent. Someone who finds us
through an ad, leaves, and returns a week later via a search was brought here by
the ad, and overwriting it would credit the wrong thing.

If you create an account, these values are copied onto your user record so we
can connect a campaign to a customer. If you never sign up, the cookie expires
after 90 days and that is the end of it.

## What it is not

`ov_attr` cannot follow you to other websites. It is readable only on
`omnia-voice.com` and its subdomains, which is how the marketing site and the
dashboard share it without any cross-domain tracking script. We do not sell it,
share it with advertising networks, or use it to build a profile of you.

## Changing your mind

Use the control above — it takes effect immediately, and turning the cookie off
deletes it. You can also clear cookies for this site in your browser, which
resets everything including the record of your answer, so you will be asked
again on your next visit.
